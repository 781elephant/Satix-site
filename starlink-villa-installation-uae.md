# Starlink installation for villas in the UAE

Satix supports customer owned Starlink installations for villas, compounds, farmhouses and residential properties across the UAE.

## Villa installation priorities

Residential installations should balance a clear sky view with a clean appearance. The exterior hardware needs a secure location, while cable routing should be practical and discreet. Indoors, the router should be placed to support the rooms and outdoor areas where reliable connectivity matters most.

## Indoor coverage

Large villas often need more than one WiFi access point. Satix can help plan router placement, mesh WiFi coverage and bridge solutions to reduce weak areas around the property.

## Typical residential work

* Dish positioning and secure mounting
* Cable routing into the property
* Router placement
* Mesh WiFi planning
* Coverage checks
* Relocation of existing equipment
