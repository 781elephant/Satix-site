# Starlink business backup connectivity in the UAE

Satix supports businesses that want customer owned Starlink equipment incorporated into a broader backup connectivity setup.

## Why businesses consider a backup connection

Offices, retail locations, hospitality operations, warehouses and temporary sites may need a second connectivity path when their primary connection is unavailable or delayed. The right design depends on the existing router, network equipment and operational requirements.

## Installation and network planning

Satix can support the physical installation as well as the local network planning around it. This can include equipment mounting, cable routing, router placement, network integration planning and testing.

## Typical business environments

* Offices
* Warehouses and logistics facilities
* Retail locations
* Hospitality properties
* Construction and temporary sites
* Remote operating locations
