# Starlink UAE license update for 2026

The UAE Telecommunications and Digital Government Regulatory Authority announced on 28 August 2026 that it granted Starlink Satellite Communications LLC a ten year General Space Services License authorizing the company to establish, operate and manage a public satellite communications network and provide broadband satellite internet services in the United Arab Emirates.

The announcement is an important development for satellite connectivity in the UAE. Installation requirements still depend on the customer equipment, property, site access, mounting location and indoor network needs.

Satix provides independent installation and connectivity support for customer owned equipment. Satix does not sell Starlink subscriptions and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Starlink or SpaceX.

Official source: [UAE TDRA announcement](https://tdra.gov.ae/en/media/press-release/2026/new-press-release)
