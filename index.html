FAQ

Before you book.

Common questions about independent Starlink installation and connectivity setup in the UAE.

Do you sell Starlink service or subscriptions?

No. Satix provides independent installation, setup and connectivity support. Starlink hardware and service plans are purchased from Starlink or another authorised source.

Can you install a kit I already own?

Yes. We can assess the site, install or relocate the equipment, route the cabling and optimise indoor connectivity.

Can Starlink be used as backup internet for a business?

It can be incorporated into a broader business continuity setup. The right configuration depends on your existing router, network and operational requirements.

Do you improve WiFi coverage inside the property?

Yes. We can plan router placement, mesh coverage and bridge solutions so the connection reaches the areas where it is needed.

Do you work outside Dubai?

Yes. Satix supports projects across all seven Emirates.
