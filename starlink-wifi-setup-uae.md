# Starlink WiFi setup and optimisation in the UAE

A strong satellite connection is only useful when the indoor network reaches the places where people actually need to work, stream and communicate.

Satix supports WiFi planning for customer owned Starlink installations across the UAE.

## Router placement matters

The router should be positioned to reduce unnecessary obstacles and place the signal closer to the areas that need coverage. Large villas, offices and warehouses may need additional access points, mesh units or bridge solutions.

## WiFi optimisation can include

* Router placement review
* Mesh WiFi planning
* Coverage checks
* Dead zone reduction
* Bridge planning between areas or buildings
* Troubleshooting weak indoor coverage
* Repositioning equipment after a property change

## From outdoor signal to indoor coverage

Satix looks at the installation as one complete path, from the exterior equipment and cable route through to the indoor network. That helps avoid a situation where the satellite connection is working correctly but the property still has poor usable coverage.
