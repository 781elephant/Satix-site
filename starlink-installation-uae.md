# Independent Starlink installation across the UAE

Satix provides installation support for customer owned Starlink equipment across Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, Ras Al Khaimah, Fujairah and Umm Al Quwain.

## Professional installation from exterior equipment to indoor coverage

A reliable satellite internet setup depends on more than placing a dish outdoors. The mounting position needs a clear view of the sky, the hardware needs to be secure, the cable route should be clean and protected, and the indoor router needs to be positioned where it can deliver useful coverage.

Satix can support new installations, relocations and troubleshooting for existing setups.

## Typical installation work

* Site review and recommended dish position
* Roof, wall or suitable exterior mounting
* Clean and practical cable routing
* Router placement and initial configuration
* Mesh WiFi planning for larger properties
* Testing after installation
* Reinstallation or relocation of existing equipment

## Properties and sites

Support is available for villas, offices, warehouses, construction sites, hospitality locations and remote properties throughout the United Arab Emirates.
